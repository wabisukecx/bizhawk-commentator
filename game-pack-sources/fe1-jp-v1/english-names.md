## Enemies

The game gives enemies names too (its second name table, person IDs 129-197): generic soldiers are named after their country ("Galda soldier", ID 133), bosses and recruitable characters have their own names. English rule: a character who is also in our army keeps that character's English name; a few bosses use the well-known name; "…へい" becomes "<romanized> soldier"; everything else is romanized.
